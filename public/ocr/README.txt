Local OCR assets for Portuguese roster imports.

tesseract-core-lstm.wasm.js: tesseract.js-core, Apache-2.0.
por.traineddata: https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast/blob/main/por.traineddata
Portuguese model distributed under Apache-2.0.

Documents are processed in the browser. These assets are loaded only when a scanned PDF needs OCR.
